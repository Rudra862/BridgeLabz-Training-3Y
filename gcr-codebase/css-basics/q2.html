About Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital city of Rajasthan and is popularly known as the Pink City. It is famous for its historical buildings, forts and colorful culture.

Famous Places

Jaipur has many famous attractions including Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace and Jantar Mantar.

Culture

The city is known for traditional music, dance, food, handicrafts and festivals.

Rajasthani Food

Popular dishes include Dal Baati Churma, Ghevar, Gatte ki Sabzi and many traditional sweets.
